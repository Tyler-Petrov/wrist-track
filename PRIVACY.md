# WristTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

WristTrack is an independent, serverless Zepp OS client for Solidtime. It is
not affiliated with or endorsed by solidtime or Zepp Health.

## Data handling

- The app asks the user for a personal Solidtime API token and, optionally,
  the address of a self-hosted Solidtime server.
- The token is stored by the Zepp mobile app's Settings Storage on the user's
  paired phone. It is not copied to the watch.
- The phone-side service sends the token only to the Solidtime server the
  user chose (`https://app.solidtime.io` unless they entered their own) to
  retrieve and modify the user's Solidtime time entries.
- Timer descriptions, project names, start times, and identifiers needed to
  render and control the timer are sent over Bluetooth between the paired
  phone and watch.
- WristTrack has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, or
  third-party tracking and does not collect or retain user data externally.

## Deletion

Users can select "Disconnect and delete token" in WristTrack's Zepp settings.
Uninstalling WristTrack also removes its local settings. Users may rotate their
personal token from their Solidtime profile at any time.

## Third parties

Use of the hosted Solidtime service is governed by Solidtime's own privacy
policy and terms; a self-hosted server is governed by whoever operates it. Use of
the Zepp app and Amazfit device is governed by Zepp Health's privacy policy and
terms.
