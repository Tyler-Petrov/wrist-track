# Zepp Store Listing Draft

## Name

WristTrack

## Short description

Start and stop your Solidtime timer from an Amazfit Bip Max.

## Full description

WristTrack puts the essential Solidtime timer controls on your wrist. View
the running entry, elapsed time, and project; stop it with one tap; or browse
your past month of recordings and start any of them again. A second tab, one
swipe to the left, totals the calendar week, today, and your payroll cycle, each
broken down by project.

The integration is serverless. Your paired phone communicates directly with
Solidtime, hosted or self-hosted, and your personal token is never sent to the watch or
to a developer-operated server. A paired phone with internet access is
required.

WristTrack is an independent client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
solidtime or Zepp Health.

Productivity

## Permissions

## Review notes

The app requires the tester to paste a Solidtime API token, created under API
Tokens on the Solidtime profile page, in the Zepp app's WristTrack settings. A
free account at https://app.solidtime.io is enough to test with.
